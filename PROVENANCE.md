## Pictures from the live site

410 pictures published on the practice's site are used. Most stand on the page where the live site shows them. The redesign also shows a page's pictures on the cards, bands and menus that lead to that page, so 4 of them are in the header menus of every page (2 of those are the practice's own photographs: its optical and waiting area, the sign in its reception). 3 of the practice's own photographs (its team outside the building, its optical and waiting area, the sign in its reception) also lead 8 pages that have another picture, or none, in that place on the live site (`/testimonial-page/`, `/contact-us/testimonials/`, `/testimonial/would-recommend/`, `/hours-location/`, `/location/york-eye-associates-pc/`, `/contact-us/`, `/the-staff/`, `/our-eye-doctors/`), and the sign in its reception also stands in the opening of the home page, where the live site does not show it. The eye mark in the header and the footer is the practice's site icon, converted by this build to navy and to white (2 of the 410); it is not a new drawing. The pictures are: the practice's own photographs, the stock photographs and diagrams of its articles, and logos from its web platform's shared catalogues. 56 could not be downloaded (55 refused with HTTP 403, 1 gone with HTTP 404) and are not here.
