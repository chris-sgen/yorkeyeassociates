Eye Care Services

Advanced Eye Care Services

Being diagnosed with an eye condition like cataracts, glaucoma, macular degeneration, or diabetic retinopathy can feel overwhelming.

You may not know what to expect or how to move forward — and that’s where we come in.

We provide compassionate, quality eye care tailored to protect and preserve your long-term vision.

  • 01

    Comprehensive Eye Exams

    Complete eye exams are not just for updating your prescription. Learn more about what your eye doctor will screen for, what to expect at your appointment, some of the advanced technology we use, and special considerations for pediatric and infant eye care.

    Learn More
  • 02

    Dry Eye

    Even without knowing the term "dry eye" or "Dry Eye Syndrome", if you suffer from it, then you are all too familiar with the soreness, discomfort, and even the constant misery that can come with dry eyes.

    Learn More
  • 03

    Myopia Management

    As a child quickly develops and the myopia rapidly progresses, the child is at a higher risk of developing dangerous eye diseases later in life, such as retinal detachment, macular degeneration, glaucoma and cataracts. Learn how myopia management can help.

    Learn More
  • 04

    Eye Disease Diagnosis and Management

    Is your vision blurred or cloudy? Seeing dark spaces in the center of your vision? You might have an eye disease. Contact us for effective treatment and experience a heightened quality of life.

    Learn More
  • 05

    Contact Lens Exams

    A contact lens exam provides all of the measurements and testing that are required to determine if your eyes are suitable for contact lens wear. We are able to fit most patients (even hard-to-fit) and will generate your contact lens prescription.

    Learn More
  • 06

    Low Vision

    Low vision is the term used to describe reduced eyesight that cannot be fully corrected with eyeglasses, contact lenses or eye surgery.

    Learn More

Myopia Management

Vision Care for Myopia Control In Gainesville

Myopia, or nearsightedness, is becoming more common in children and often worsens as they grow. We provide treatment options designed to slow its progression and protect their long-term vision.

Now Offering Stellest™ Lenses
Our new Stellest™ lenses use innovative technology to manage myopia while providing clear, comfortable vision. They're an excellent choice for parents seeking a non-invasive, effective solution for their child’s eye growth.
